# Retail Intelligence & Customer Analytics | Python

## Project Overview

A Python-based exploratory data analysis (EDA) project focused on understanding customer purchasing behavior, demographic segments, and retail sales performance. The objective was to identify key sales drivers and translate data-driven findings into actionable recommendations for customer targeting, inventory planning, and business growth.

## Tech Stack

**Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Jupyter Notebook**

## Dataset & Preparation

- Analyzed **11,251 initial records**, retaining **11,239 records** after data cleaning.
- Handled missing values, removed irrelevant columns, and prepared the dataset for analysis.
- Analyzed **3,752 distinct customers** and approximately **106.25 million in recorded sales**.

## Key Areas of Analysis

- **Customer Segmentation:** Analyzed sales across gender, age groups, occupation, and marital status.
- **Geographic Analysis:** Compared sales contributions across states.
- **Product Performance:** Evaluated product categories and ranked products by order volume.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Used aggregations and visualizations to identify purchasing patterns and high-contributing segments.

## Key Findings

| Area | Key Insight |
|---|---|
| Gender | Female customers contributed approximately **70% of recorded sales**. |
| Age Group | Customers aged **26–35** contributed approximately **40% of recorded sales**. |
| Product Categories | Food, Clothing & Apparel, and Electronics & Gadgets collectively contributed approximately **62% of recorded sales**. |
| Geography | Uttar Pradesh was the highest sales-contributing state, followed by Maharashtra and Karnataka. |
| Occupation | IT-sector customers were the largest sales-contributing occupation group. |

*Sales figures represent recorded sales amounts, not profit. The currency unit should be verified against the original dataset source.*

## Business Recommendations

- **Targeted Marketing:** Develop customer-specific campaigns based on high-contributing demographic segments and their product preferences.
- **Inventory Optimization:** Use category-level sales and order trends to guide stock planning, subject to inventory and profitability data.
- **Regional Strategy:** Investigate high-contributing states for localized marketing and inventory allocation.
- **Performance Measurement:** Track conversion, repeat purchases, and campaign performance to validate whether proposed strategies improve business outcomes.
